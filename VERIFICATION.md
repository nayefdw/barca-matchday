## Version 2.3.1 fixes

The seven reported issues were addressed:

- Notification subscription starts synchronously from the Enable tap after setup is prepared. Setup, subscription and save waits are bounded; blocked permissions, unsupported browsers and retry states receive visible guidance. Reminder freshness now shares the fixture refresher’s hourly/six-hour cadence. Automated checks cover the tap timing, timeout/error messages and valid six-hour schedules. Real iPhone notification delivery remains untested.
- Player aliases now resolve exact football biographies, reject disambiguation pages and use verified Wikidata image references when a page thumbnail is missing. The saved 28-player roster has 24 credited, verified portraits; four youth players without a verified suitable image retain the shirt fallback. Automated checks cover aliases, goalkeeper biographies, disambiguation rejection and Wikidata portrait recovery.
- Recent meetings can supplement an empty or limited free response with verified UEFA and official FC Barcelona archive facts for mapped opponents. Dates, scores and competition labels retain source links; date-only records do not invent kickoff times. Tests cover an empty successful provider response, archive merging, provider priority, unknown opponents and a reserved Champions League warming slot. Archive coverage is limited and does not promise five meetings for every opponent.
- Explicit match venues take priority. A separately fetched club ground is saved even when no stadium image exists, and is labelled “Home ground” when the fixture omits its venue. The detail sheet explains that a venue change remains possible. Tests confirm historical artwork is never treated as venue confirmation.
- Pointer interaction no longer leaves blue focus lines or a blue ring on the close button. Noninteractive main/dialog containers do not receive outlines; keyboard controls retain a visible focus indicator.
- The Previous / Then / After strip has centred larger crests, a separate score/badge row and balanced text spacing. The three-column layout was visually verified at 390px and 320px, replacing the earlier narrow left-aligned rows.
- Refresh saved fixtures displays progress and a completion time on success, with a persistent Settings status and toast. Failure/offline states explicitly say previously saved fixtures are being shown. This reloads the latest server snapshot; it does not bypass scheduled provider polling.

The public browser displayed the real home poster and both standings tables at publication. Missing player photographs use the existing shirt fallback, and unsupported grounds use the stadium illustration.
